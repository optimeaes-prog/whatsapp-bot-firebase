# Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) — Proplead

**Last updated:** 25 May 2026  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Applicable to:** Proplead customers (real estate agencies, freelance agents and business entities)

> This Acceptable Use Policy ("**AUP**") forms an integral part of the **Terms and Conditions** of Proplead and is binding on all customers. A breach of this AUP constitutes a **material breach** of the Terms and Conditions and may result in immediate suspension or termination of access to the Platform.

---

## 1. Purpose and scope

This AUP sets out the rules of conduct and acceptable use of the Proplead Platform, including its WhatsApp messaging features, AI-powered lead automation, voice call management, listing management and CRM tools.

The purpose of this AUP is to:
- Protect Platform users, leads/contacts and third parties
- Ensure compliance with the policies of integrated service providers (WhatsApp, Twilio, OpenAI, etc.)
- Safeguard the integrity, availability and reputation of the Platform
- Provide a clear contractual basis for action in the event of abuse

This AUP applies to all customers, their authorised users and anyone accessing the Platform under their credentials.

---

## 2. Use of WhatsApp and messaging channels

### 2.1 Contacting leads

The customer may only contact individuals via WhatsApp who:
- Have demonstrated a prior, verifiable interest in real estate properties (inbound leads), or
- Have given informed consent to be contacted via messaging channels, or
- Have an existing contractual or pre-contractual relationship with the customer

Sending bulk messages to contact databases acquired without individual prior consent or without a valid legal basis under applicable electronic communications law is prohibited.

### 2.2 Identification and accuracy

The customer must:
- Clearly identify themselves as a real estate agent or company in the first message
- Not impersonate any person, company, property portal or entity
- Not mislead the recipient about the nature of the contact, the property offered, or the terms and conditions
- Indicate, where relevant, that automated or AI tools are being used to manage the conversation

### 2.3 Respecting opt-outs

When a lead expresses a wish not to be contacted — whether through words such as "STOP", "unsubscribe", "don't contact me", "not interested" or any equivalent expression — the customer must:
- Immediately cease all commercial communications
- Record the opt-out preference in the system
- Not contact that person again for commercial purposes

Failure to respect opt-outs may constitute a violation of the UK/EU GDPR and applicable electronic marketing regulations.

### 2.4 Timing and frequency

Commercial messages sent via the Platform should be sent at reasonable times. As a (non-exhaustive) guideline, it is recommended not to send unsolicited messages between 21:00 and 09:00 local time for the recipient, or to accumulate more than 3–4 unanswered messages to the same contact within a 48-hour period.

### 2.5 WhatsApp Business and Twilio policies

The customer agrees to comply at all times with the WhatsApp Business API policies and Twilio's Acceptable Use Policy (Proplead's messaging provider). In particular:
- Not use message templates for purposes not authorised by the channel
- Not send messages that breach Meta's content policies (violence, adult content, fraud, etc.)
- Comply with consent requirements for sending marketing messages via WhatsApp

Non-compliance with WhatsApp/Twilio policies may result in the customer's phone number being suspended by those providers, an action over which Talmate has no control.

## 3. Use of Artificial Intelligence features

### 3.1 Mandatory supervision

The Platform incorporates AI-powered lead qualification flows and automated responses (using OpenAI language models). The customer acknowledges that:
- AI outputs may contain errors, inaccuracies or inappropriate responses
- **The customer is responsible for supervising** automated conversations and AI-generated lead summaries
- Internal processes must be in place to review AI outputs before making material decisions about leads

### 3.2 Specific AI prohibitions

The following are prohibited:
- **Attempting to manipulate or deceive the AI system** (jailbreaking, prompt injection or other techniques to obtain unauthorised outputs)
- **Entering special category data** (health, sexual orientation, political opinions, racial origin, etc.) into conversations or Platform fields, unless strictly necessary with a valid lawful basis and appropriate safeguards
- **Entering credentials, passwords or sensitive security information** into conversation fields or lead notes
- Using AI outputs as **legal, financial, medical or investment advice** or presenting them as such to leads
- Relying on AI summaries for high-impact decisions without prior human validation

### 3.3 AI-related complaints

If a lead submits a complaint relating to an automated communication, the customer is responsible for handling it. Talmate will provide support information where appropriate, but responsibility towards the lead rests with the customer as Data Controller.

## 4. Call recordings

### 4.1 Obligation to inform

When the customer enables call recording through Proplead, they are required to:
- Inform the person being called, before or at the start of the call, that the conversation may be recorded
- State the purpose of the recording and the retention period
- Obtain prior consent where required by applicable law (in Spain, an informative notice may be sufficient unless the recording is used for purposes requiring explicit consent)

We recommend using the notice templates available in `legal/call-recording-notice.es.md`.

### 4.2 Use of recordings

Recordings may only be used for the purposes communicated to the data subject. The following are prohibited:
- Sharing recordings with unauthorised third parties
- Using recordings to train proprietary AI models without a lawful basis
- Retaining recordings beyond the period notified to the data subject

## 5. Data protection and privacy

### 5.1 Lawful basis

The customer, as Data Controller for their leads' and contacts' data, must:
- Have a **valid lawful basis** (consent, legitimate interests, contract performance, etc.) for contacting each lead and processing their data
- Have an up-to-date privacy notice informing leads about the processing of their data
- Use the privacy notice template available in `legal/lead-notice-template.es.md` or an equivalent GDPR-compliant notice

### 5.2 Personal data prohibitions

The following are prohibited:
- **Uploading lead databases** obtained without individual consent or without a valid lawful basis
- **Processing special category personal data** (health, ideology, sexual orientation, biometric data, etc.) through the Platform, unless strictly necessary with a valid lawful basis and appropriate safeguards
- **Storing data of minors** (persons under 14 in Spain / under 16 in other contexts) on the Platform without the specific safeguards and lawful bases required
- Using the Platform to process personal data for purposes other than managing real estate leads and communicating with prospective clients

### 5.3 Data subject rights

The customer is responsible for handling rights requests (access, rectification, erasure, objection, portability) sent directly to them by their leads. Talmate will assist the customer to the extent set out in the DPA.

## 6. Account security

The customer agrees to:
- **Not share credentials** between different users — each authorised user must have their own access
- Use strong passwords and, where available, **multi-factor authentication (MFA)**
- **Notify Talmate without delay** (soporte@proplead.io) of any unauthorised access, credential theft or security incident related to their account
- Not attempt to access data belonging to other Platform customers
- Not conduct vulnerability testing, port scanning or attacks of any kind against Proplead's infrastructure
- Ensure that authorised users are aware of and comply with this AUP

## 7. Expressly prohibited uses

In addition to the above, the following uses of the Platform are expressly prohibited:

### 7.1 Illegal or fraudulent activities
- Any activity that breaches applicable UK, EU or other law
- Fraud, scams, identity theft or deception
- Money laundering or other criminal activities

### 7.2 Prohibited content
- Spam, unsolicited bulk communications or chain messages
- Phishing or impersonation of official bodies, banks or trusted entities
- Content promoting or facilitating violence, hatred, discrimination, pornography or other illegal content
- Content that infringes the intellectual property rights of third parties

### 7.3 Platform abuse
- Mass data extraction (scraping) from the Platform using unauthorised automated means
- Reselling, sublicensing or providing access to the Platform to unauthorised third parties
- Using the Platform to provide services to third parties without Talmate's express written authorisation
- Attempting to gain access to unsubscribed features or other customers' data
- Deliberately overloading the Platform's infrastructure

### 7.4 Breach of provider policies
- Any action that breaches the terms and policies of Meta (WhatsApp Business API), Twilio, OpenAI, Stripe or other providers integrated into the Platform
- The customer is responsible for being aware of and complying with such third-party terms to the extent they are relevant to their use of Proplead

## 8. Consequences of non-compliance

A breach of this AUP may result in:

1. **Formal warning** — Talmate will notify the customer of the breach and provide a remedy period (where reasonable)
2. **Temporary suspension** of Platform access — without prior notice if there is a serious, imminent or ongoing risk to third parties, the Platform or its users
3. **Termination of contract** — with immediate effect for serious or repeated breaches, in accordance with the Terms and Conditions
4. **Claim for damages** — Talmate may claim direct damages caused by the breach from the customer, including penalties or blocks imposed by service providers (WhatsApp, Twilio, etc.) as a result of the abuse
5. **Reporting to authorities** — in the event of suspected illegal activities, Talmate may report them to the competent authorities and cooperate with their investigations
