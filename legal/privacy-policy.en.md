# Privacy Policy — Talmate Limited (Proplead)

**Last updated:** 25 May 2026
**Version:** 1.0

---

This Privacy Policy explains how **Talmate Limited** (“**Talmate**”, “**we**”, “**us**”) processes personal data in connection with the **Proplead** platform (www.proplead.io).

Talmate may act in two distinct roles depending on the context:

- **Data Controller**: when processing data about its own users, customers, website visitors, billing and marketing.
- **Data Processor**: when processing leads/contacts data and conversations on behalf of its customers (the real estate agencies and agents who use Proplead).

This policy primarily covers the **Controller** role. When acting as a Processor, the applicable framework is the Data Processing Agreement (DPA) signed with each customer.

> **Note for real estate leads and contacts:** If a real estate agent contacted you through Proplead and you wish to exercise your data rights (access, erasure, etc.), please contact that agent or agency directly. Talmate acts as a Processor in that context, and the Controller is the Proplead customer.

## 3. Roles: Controller and Processor

### 3.1 When Talmate acts as a Controller

Talmate is a Controller when it determines the purposes and means of processing, which occurs in connection with:

- Creating and managing user accounts on the platform (Firebase Auth).
- Managing licences, subscriptions and credits.
- Billing and collections via Stripe.
- System security and fraud prevention.
- Talmate’s own marketing: campaigns, newsletters, advertising.
- Cookies and tracking technologies on the website and app.
- Support, sales and demo communications.

### 3.2 When Talmate acts as a Processor

Talmate acts as a Processor when, under instruction and on behalf of its customers (the real estate agencies), it processes:

- WhatsApp conversations between the agent and their leads.
- Lead/contact data belonging to the customer (name, phone, email, property preferences, etc.).
- AI-driven lead qualification workflows and summary generation.
- Call recordings or recording URLs, where the customer has enabled this feature.
- Any other personal data contained in messages exchanged via the platform.

In this case, the **Controller** is the Proplead customer (the real estate agency or agent), and individuals whose data is processed should direct their rights requests to that customer. Talmate will assist the customer in accordance with the applicable DPA.

## 5. Purposes and lawful bases for processing

The following table sets out the purposes for which Talmate processes personal data as a Controller, together with the corresponding lawful basis under Article 6 EU GDPR (and equivalent UK GDPR provisions):

| Purpose | Lawful basis (Article 6 GDPR) |
|---|---|
| Account creation and management; provision of the contracted service | Art. 6(1)(b) — performance of a contract or pre-contractual steps |
| Billing, collections and subscription management | Art. 6(1)(b) performance of a contract; Art. 6(1)(c) compliance with a legal obligation (accounting, tax) |
| Technical support and customer service | Art. 6(1)(b) performance of a contract; Art. 6(1)(f) legitimate interests in providing adequate support |

Where the lawful basis is **legitimate interests** (Art. 6(1)(f)), Talmate has conducted or will conduct a balancing test to ensure that our interests are not overridden by the rights and freedoms of the individuals concerned.

Where the lawful basis is **consent** (Art. 6(1)(a)), you have the right to withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out prior to withdrawal. Withdrawal of consent does not mean we will stop providing the service, unless the processing is strictly necessary for it.

## 7. International data transfers

Talmate uses global providers, which may involve transfers of personal data outside the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom (UK). The applicable safeguards are as follows:

### 7.1 Transfers from the EU/EEA to the United Kingdom

Since January 2021, the United Kingdom is treated as a **third country** for EU GDPR purposes. The European Commission adopted an **Adequacy Decision** in respect of the UK in June 2021. Talmate relies on that decision for transfers of personal data from the EU/EEA to the United Kingdom.

> **Important note:** The EU-UK Adequacy Decision was adopted with an automatic review clause. Talmate actively monitors the status of that decision and, should it lapse or be revoked, will promptly implement **EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)** or another valid transfer mechanism to ensure continuity of protection.

### 7.2 Transfers from the UK to the United States and other third countries

The providers OpenAI, Stripe and Calendly are established in the United States. For transfers from the United Kingdom to these providers, Talmate uses:

- **UK International Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA)**, or
- **UK IDTA Addendum** to the EU SCCs, as appropriate.

### 7.3 Transfers from the EU/EEA to the United States and other third countries

For the processing of data of EU/EEA residents by providers established in the US (Google, Stripe, OpenAI, Calendly), Talmate relies on the **EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)** adopted by the European Commission in June 2021, including the relevant modules (Controller to Processor, or Processor to Sub-processor).

You may request further information about the applicable transfer safeguards by writing to soporte@proplead.io.

## 8. Retention periods

Talmate retains personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, unless applicable law requires or permits a longer period:

| Data category | Indicative retention period |
|---|---|
| Account and authentication data | Duration of the account + 2 additional years after closure (to address potential claims) |
| Billing data, contracts and payment history | 7 years from the end of the relevant financial year (tax obligation; the more stringent period between UK — 6 years — and Spain — 7 years — applies) |
| Security and access logs | 90 days on a rolling basis |
| Support communications | Up to 2 years after case resolution, unless litigation is pending |
| Marketing preferences data | Until unsubscription or withdrawal of consent |
| Analytics data (consent-based) | Per tool configuration; typically 14 months for GA4 (default setting) |
| Lead/conversation data (Processor role) | Per customer instructions and DPA; by default, deletion or anonymisation after 3 years of inactivity unless instructed otherwise |

Once retention periods expire, data is securely deleted or anonymised so that re-identification is not possible.

## 9. Security

Talmate implements appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, loss, accidental disclosure or destruction. These measures include, among others:

- Role-based access control (RBAC) and multi-factor authentication (MFA) for internal systems.
- Encryption in transit (TLS/HTTPS) and at rest for data stored on Firebase/Google Cloud.
- Audit logs and monitoring for suspicious activity.
- Use of certified providers (Google Cloud and Stripe hold ISO 27001, SOC 2 and PCI-DSS certifications as applicable).
- Restriction of employee and contractor access to the minimum necessary for their roles (least-privilege principle).
- Documented incident response and security breach procedures.

In the event of a personal data breach that is likely to result in a risk to the rights and freedoms of individuals, Talmate will notify the competent supervisory authorities within the legally prescribed timeframes (72 hours for the ICO and/or the AEPD, as applicable) and will notify affected individuals where required.

## 10. Your rights as a data subject

Under the EU GDPR and UK GDPR, you have the following rights in respect of personal data for which Talmate acts as a Controller:

### 10.1 Right of access (Article 15 GDPR)

You may request confirmation of whether we process your personal data and, if so, obtain a copy together with information about the purposes, categories, recipients and retention periods.

### 10.2 Right to rectification (Article 16 GDPR)

You may request the correction of inaccurate personal data or the completion of incomplete data.

### 10.3 Right to erasure / “right to be forgotten” (Article 17 GDPR)

You may request the erasure of your data where, among other reasons, it is no longer necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, you have withdrawn consent, or you have objected and there are no overriding legitimate grounds. This right may be subject to limitations where processing is necessary for compliance with a legal obligation or for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.

### 10.4 Right to restriction of processing (Article 18 GDPR)

In certain circumstances (e.g., while the accuracy of data is being verified or an objection is being assessed), you may request that processing be restricted to mere storage.

### 10.5 Right to data portability (Article 20 GDPR)

Where processing is based on consent or contract performance and is carried out by automated means, you may request to receive your data in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format, and to have it transmitted to another controller where technically feasible.

### 10.6 Right to object (Article 21 GDPR)

You may object at any time to processing based on Talmate’s legitimate interests, including direct marketing. Where you object to direct marketing, we will cease processing your data for that purpose immediately.

### 10.7 Rights related to automated decision-making and profiling (Article 22 GDPR)

Proplead does not currently make automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects on platform users. Automated processing of lead conversations is subject to the customer’s DPA.

### 10.8 Right to withdraw consent

Where processing is based on your consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing prior to withdrawal. You may withdraw cookie consent via the preferences panel on our website, and withdraw marketing consent via the unsubscribe link or by contacting us.

### 10.9 Right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

- **For residents of Spain or the EU:** you may lodge a complaint with the **Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (AEPD)** (www.aepd.es) or the supervisory authority of your habitual place of residence.
- **For residents of the United Kingdom:** you may lodge a complaint with the **Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO)** (www.ico.org.uk).

We nonetheless encourage you to contact us first so that we can address any privacy concern directly and promptly.

### How to exercise your rights

Send your request to **soporte@proplead.io**. We will respond within one month of receipt (extendable to three months in cases of particular complexity, with prior notification). We may request reasonable evidence of your identity where we have legitimate doubts. Exercising your rights is free of charge, except where requests are manifestly unfounded or excessive.
