## 3. Roles (Controller vs Processor)
### 3.1 When Talmate is a Controller
Talmate is a Controller for:
- account creation and access management (Firebase Auth)
- Platform user administration
- billing, payments, subscriptions and credits (Stripe)
- security and fraud/abuse prevention
- Talmate’s own marketing and analytics/ads (where implemented)

### 3.2 When Talmate is a Processor
Talmate typically acts as a Processor when, on behalf of the customer, it processes:
- WhatsApp/voice conversations with the customer’s leads/contacts
- AI-driven qualification workflows and lead summaries
- call recordings or recording URLs, where enabled

If you are a lead/contact of a customer, the Controller is the customer who contacted you. You should ask them to handle any rights requests (access, deletion, etc.). Talmate may assist the customer under the DPA.

## 4. Personal data we process as a Controller
Depending on how you use the Platform, we may process:
- **Account data**: email, UID, access credentials (via Firebase), login records.
- **Business contact data**: name, company, role, email, phone (if you provide it).
- **Billing/payment data**: Stripe customer/subscription identifiers, payment status, tokenised payment method identifiers, credit purchase history.
- **Communications**: support/sales messages, tickets, and diagnostic logs.
- **Marketing data**: marketing preferences and campaign metrics (where applicable).
- **Technical data**: IP address, user agent, security logs, usage events (depending on configuration).

## 5. Purposes and lawful bases (UK/EU)
We process Controller data for:
- **Providing the service and managing your account**: contract / pre-contractual steps.
- **Payments, invoicing, accounting**: contract and/or legal obligation.
- **Security, fraud prevention, abuse prevention**: legitimate interests.
- **Support**: contract / legitimate interests.
- **Talmate marketing**: consent and/or legitimate interests depending on the case and applicable law; always with an opt-out.
- **Cookies/analytics/ads**: consent for non-essential cookies and advertising technologies (see Cookies Policy).

We may share data with vendors to operate the Platform, for example:

Exact periods will be detailed in a Retention Policy (see `legal/`).
