## 1. What are cookies and similar technologies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by a website or app. They allow the service to remember information about your visit (session, preferences, activity) so it works correctly, or to measure and personalise your experience.

In addition to cookies, Proplead may use similar technologies such as:
- **Third-party SDKs and scripts** (e.g., tracking pixels, Google Tag Manager)
- **Browser local storage** (localStorage/sessionStorage)
- **Session identifiers** in the web application

In this policy, we refer to all these technologies collectively as "cookies".

## 4. Categories of cookies we use

### 4.1 Strictly necessary cookies

These are essential for the Platform to function. Without them, you cannot log in or use basic features. **No consent required** (PECR Reg. 6(4); Art. 6(1)(b)/(f) UK/EU GDPR).

| Cookie / Technology | Provider | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| Firebase session token | Google LLC / Firebase | Authentication and login | Session / until sign-out |
| Firebase Auth refresh token | Google LLC / Firebase | Automatic session renewal | Persistent (until sign-out or expiry) |
| `__session` | Talmate / Firebase | App session state | Session |
| Security / anti-CSRF cookies | Talmate | Fraud and CSRF attack prevention | Session |

### 4.2 Analytics and measurement cookies

These help us understand how the site and Platform are used (pages visited, events, performance) to improve our service. **Prior consent required** in UK/EEA.

| Cookie | Provider | Purpose | Duration | Country | Transfer mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `_ga` | Google LLC (GA4) | Identify unique user sessions | 2 years | USA | UK IDTA / EU SCCs |
| `_ga_[ID]` | Google LLC (GA4) | Store GA4 session state | 2 years | USA | UK IDTA / EU SCCs |
| `_gid` | Google LLC (GA4) | Distinguish unique users | 24 hours | USA | UK IDTA / EU SCCs |

**Google Analytics 4 (GA4):** We use GA4 to measure user behaviour on the site and app. Data is anonymised where possible. Data retention is set to 14 months. Privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

**Google Tag Manager:** We use GTM to manage third-party tags and scripts. GTM itself does not collect personal data; it only fires vendor scripts when you have given consent for the relevant category.

### 4.3 Advertising and retargeting cookies

These cookies measure the effectiveness of our advertising campaigns and allow us to show Proplead ads on third-party platforms. **Prior consent required**.

| Cookie | Provider | Purpose | Duration | Country | Transfer mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `_fbp` | Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd | Identify browser for conversion measurement and retargeting on Facebook/Instagram | 3 months | Ireland / USA | EU SCCs |
| `_fbc` | Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd | Store Facebook ad click parameter | 2 years | Ireland / USA | EU SCCs |
| `_gcl_au` | Google LLC (Google Ads) | Measure Google Ads conversions and retargeting | 3 months | USA | UK IDTA / EU SCCs |
| `__gads` / `__gpi` | Google LLC | Ad frequency and campaign measurement | 13 months | USA | UK IDTA / EU SCCs |

**Meta Pixel:** The Meta Pixel measures conversions from our Facebook and Instagram ads and enables custom audience creation. More information: https://www.facebook.com/privacy/explanation

**Google Ads / Google Tag:** We use Google's conversion tag to measure which ads lead to sign-ups or purchases, and to show remarketing ads. More information: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 6. Managing your preferences

### 6.1 Consent banner

When you first visit Proplead, a cookie banner is displayed. You can:
- **Accept all** — enable analytics and advertising cookies
- **Reject** — keep only strictly necessary cookies
- **Configure** — choose which categories to enable

You can change your preferences at any time via the **"Manage cookies"** link in the site footer.

> In line with AEPD 2023 guidance, rejecting non-essential cookies is as easy as accepting them. Access to the Platform is not conditional on accepting non-essential cookies.

### 6.2 Browser settings

You can manage or delete cookies directly from your browser:
- **Chrome:** Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
- **Firefox:** Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
- **Safari:** Preferences → Privacy
- **Edge:** Settings → Privacy, search and services

Please note that blocking all cookies may affect log-in and other core features.

### 6.3 Vendor-specific opt-outs

- **Google Analytics:** Browser opt-out extension: https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout
- **Meta (Facebook):** Ad preferences in your Facebook account or https://www.facebook.com/adpreferences
- **Google Ads:** Ad settings: https://adssettings.google.com
