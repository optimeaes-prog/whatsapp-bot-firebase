# Call recording notice (template)

**Last updated:** 2026-04-01  
**Version:** 0.1 (template)  

> This template is for the **Customer** (Controller) who makes/receives calls and uses Proplead/Talmate as a provider. Adjust wording to your country and lawful basis.

## Option A — Short pre-call notice (voice)
Before we continue, please note that **this call may be recorded** for [PURPOSE, e.g., service quality and handling your enquiry].  
If you do not agree, please tell us and we will offer an alternative.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by emailing [PRIVACY_EMAIL].
