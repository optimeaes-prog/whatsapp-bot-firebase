# Privacy notice template for leads/contacts (for Proplead customers)

**Last updated:** 2026-04-01  
**Version:** 0.1 (template)  

This template helps the **Customer** (agency/freelancer) meet transparency obligations when contacting a lead via **WhatsApp** and/or **calls** using Proplead.

> Instructions: replace placeholders in brackets (`[ ... ]`) and adapt the lawful basis to your case (consent, legitimate interests, contract/pre-contract steps, etc.).

## Option A — Short WhatsApp first message
Hi, I’m [AGENT_NAME] from [COMPANY]. I’m contacting you about [PROPERTY/LISTING].  
We process your data to **handle your enquiry**, **qualify your requirements**, and **coordinate a call/visit**.  
Controller: [COMPANY] ([PRIVACY_CONTACT_EMAIL]). You can exercise your rights and read more at: [PRIVACY_POLICY_LINK].

## Option B — Longer notice (where the channel allows)
Controller: **[COMPANY]**, address [ADDRESS], privacy contact [PRIVACY_CONTACT_EMAIL].  
Purposes: (i) handle your enquiry and manage the relationship, (ii) qualify your requirements to suggest suitable properties, (iii) coordinate visits or calls, (iv) fraud/security (if applicable).  
Lawful basis: [LAWFUL_BASIS].  
Recipients: service providers (e.g., Proplead/Talmate as processor, messaging/voice providers, hosting/storage).  
International transfers: [YES/NO] (where applicable, with appropriate safeguards).  
Retention: for [PERIOD] or until [CRITERIA], then as required by law.  
Your rights: access, rectification, deletion, objection, restriction, portability (as applicable). To exercise them: [RIGHTS_CHANNEL].  
More info: [PRIVACY_POLICY_LINK].

We may use automation tools to respond faster and manage your enquiry. You can always ask to speak with a person.
